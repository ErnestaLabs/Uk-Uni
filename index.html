Student Finance England

How the money works.

The UK government covers your tuition entirely. You repay nothing until you earn over £25,000.

1

We apply with you

We walk you through the Student Finance England application. About 30 minutes. We handle the complexity so you don't have to.

2

Government pays tuition

Student Finance England pays your fees directly to the university. That money never passes through your hands.

3

Maintenance loan paid to you

You also receive a maintenance loan for living costs — paid directly to your bank each term.

ℹ️

Repayments are income-contingent. You repay 9% of earnings above £25,000 per year — only when you're earning enough to. If you never earn over the threshold, you never repay a penny. Remaining balance written off after 40 years.
