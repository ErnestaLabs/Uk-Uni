Who is this for?

Student Finance England is available to three groups of people living in the UK. Find out which applies to you.

🇬🇧

British Nationals

UK citizens and individuals with Indefinite Leave to Remain are fully entitled to Student Finance England for undergraduate and postgraduate courses.

Student Finance England

How the Funding Works

The UK government covers your tuition through Student Finance England. You pay nothing until you earn over £25,000.

1

Apply for Student Finance

We guide you through the Student Finance England application — it takes about 30 minutes online. We're with you every step.

2

Government Pays Tuition

Student Finance England pays your tuition fees directly to the university. You never handle the money yourself.

3

Receive a Maintenance Loan

You also receive a maintenance loan for living costs, paid in installments each academic term directly into your bank account.

ℹ️

Repayments are income-contingent and capped. You repay 9% of earnings above £25,000 per year — only when you're earning. If you never earn over the threshold, you never repay a penny. Outstanding balances are written off after 40 years.
